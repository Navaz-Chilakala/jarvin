# Piper voice source

Jarvin bundles the English (United States) Joe medium ONNX voice and its configuration from [Piper voices](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/joe/medium). The upstream model card identifies the voice dataset and the base-model dataset as CC0. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/joe/medium/MODEL_CARD)
